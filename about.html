About Me

Veteran, problem solver, and software engineering graduate.

My path into software began with curiosity and grew into a completed Software Engineering degree and a serious commitment to building useful, reliable technology.

My Story

Welcome to my portfolio. I am a United States Veteran who served four years in the Marine Corps. After transitioning back into civilian life, I discovered programming through Java and realized I had a strong interest in understanding how software works. I later completed my Software Engineering degree at Southern New Hampshire University with a 3.742 G.P.A.

Since then, I have continued learning multiple languages and technologies, including Java, JavaScript, C#, C++, HTML, CSS, SQL, React, jQuery, Python, Visual Basic, and Ruby.

I enjoy programming because it allows me to create tools that can improve the lives and work of others. My goal is to continue growing as a professional software engineer who builds dependable, user-focused applications.

Career Direction

I am focused on software engineering, Windows application development, database-driven tools, and clean user experiences. I want to keep building projects that demonstrate growth, technical ability, and real-world usefulness.
